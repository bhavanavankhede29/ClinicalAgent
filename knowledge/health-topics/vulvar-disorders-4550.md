# Vulvar Disorders

The vulva is the external part of a woman's genitals. Some problems you can have with the vulvar area include: Vaginitis or vulvovaginitis, swelling or infection of the vulva and vagina Skin problems due to allergy Vulvar cancer Vulvodynia, or vulvar pain Symptoms may include redness, itching, pain, or cracks in the skin. Treatment depends on the cause.

Related MedlinePlus topic groups: Women; Female Reproductive System.
